Why early detection matters

Blood cancer symptoms are easy to brush off.

Blood cancer disrupts how blood cells are produced and function, usually originating in the bone marrow or lymphatic system. Its early signs â€” fever, tiredness, frequent infections, weight loss â€” overlap with everyday illnesses, so many patients delay seeing a doctor until the disease has progressed.

Diagnosis through lab tests and specialist visits takes time and is harder to access in rural and underserved areas. UnitedCare offers a quick first opinion and a clear next step.

Leukemia

Affects the blood and bone marrow, causing abnormal white blood cell production.

Lymphoma

Affects the lymphatic system, often presenting as swollen lymph nodes.

Myeloma

Affects plasma cells in the bone marrow, frequently causing bone pain.
